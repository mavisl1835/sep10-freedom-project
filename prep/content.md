## Overview/Introduction/Context
What technology and apps/websites do nurses and doctors use? Here's a general list of what they might use.

## Part A: existing technology

##### Software

* Epocrates
  * App
  * Gives information on prescription drugs [types, how they interact with other medication]
  * Also can identify medication
  * Available on iOS and Android
  * Free, with an upgrade for disease and diagnostic access, or a subscription

* Med Mnemonics
  * App
  * Provides over 1,500 acrynoms, rhymes, and memory tricks to help nurses learn medical terminology for diseases, symptoms and more
  * Available on iOS and Android
  * Nominal fee

* NurseGrid
  * Helps nurses create a scheduel and keep track of tasks
  * Available on iOS and through a web portal
  * Free
