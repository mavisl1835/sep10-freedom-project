# Content
Topic: **Medical Practice**

## Overview/Introduction/Context
What technology and apps/websites do nurses and doctors use? Here's a general list of what they might use.

## Part A: existing technology

##### Software

* Epocrates
  * App
  * Gives information on prescription drugs [types, how they interact with other medication]
  * Also can identify medication
  * Available on iOS and Android
  * Free, with an upgrade for disease and diagnostic access, or a subscription

* Med Mnemonics
  * App
  * Provides over 1,500 acrynoms, rhymes, and memory tricks to help nurses learn medical terminology for diseases, symptoms and more
  * Available on iOS and Android
  * Nominal fee

* NurseGrid
  * Helps nurses create a scheduel and keep track of tasks
  * Available on iOS and through a web portal
  * Free
##### Hardware
* ECG/EKG Machine
  * Monitors and records the electrical activity of the heart
  * Can be used to detect heart attacks and arrhythmias [irregular heartbeat]
  * [Example of an EKG/ECG machine](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcR2G7ajkTtTjwA0k4_efuHNKVVEv0Pc4XHgA8y2kgFwLzDfuYlLmqymI0Yc&s=10)

* Pulse Oximeter
  * Looks like a clip
  * Is attached to the finger, toe, or earlobe
  * Measures oxygen saturation of the blood [how much oxygen is in your blood]
  * [Example of a pulse oximeter](https://ysm-res.cloudinary.com/image/upload/ar_1:1,c_fill,g_faces:auto,w_auto/q_auto:eco/dpr_auto/f_auto/c_scale/v1/yms/prod/9f962a05-1975-4361-8f63-ce3eaf7610c0?_a=DAJKG5DbZAAA)

* Ventilator
  * Used to help people breathe [whether they're in the ICU, or in surgery]
  * Complicated looking
  * [Example of a ventilator](https://media-cldnry.s-nbcnews.com/image/upload/newscms/2020_13/3283096/200325-ventilator-al-0929.jpg)
 
* Infusion Pump
  * Used to deliver fluids, medication, and/or nutrients directly into the veins at a specific rate and amount
  * Looks like an IV stand, but with a device attached to it
  * [Example of an infusion pump](https://wtcs.pressbooks.pub/app/uploads/sites/29/2020/11/DSC_0738-e1443533768679-scaled-1-678x1024.jpg)
