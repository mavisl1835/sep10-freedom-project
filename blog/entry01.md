## Choosing a Topic.
The topic I ultimately decided on is medical practice. The other two options I had were military and cooking. I actually wanted to do military but after going through more research, I found that medical practice was more interesting than the other two topics. I've been interested in the medical field ever since I was little, as little as pre-k, wanting to be a dentist which I think is a category in the medical field. Although I'm not a fan of the more graphic side of the medical field, I find the terms and technology the medical industry uses fascinating.

Some sources I used include

* [What is Medical Technology?](https://www.medtecheurope.org/about-the-industry/what-is-medical-technology/)
* [Military Technology: A New Era of Defense to Protect Our Troops](https://www.captechu.edu/blog/military-technology-new-era-of-defense-protect-our-troops)
* [The Future of Cooking: Smart Kitchen Equipment and Gadgets](https://gourmetkitchenworks.com/blogs/recipes/the-future-of-cooking-smart-kitchen-equipment-and-gadgets?srsltid=AYCX9vKVd1LOpFGMNOr26iPCPr1GTDewgaluC0gQTEPtIE3QVspZbkS5)
