## Engineering Design Process [EDP]
I'm currently on the first step of the Engineering Design Process which is defining a problem. The problem is that **not a lot of people realize just how much medical technology can show up in daily lives, and that they're not limited to the hospital setting.** The next step is to do research on the problem using articles and websites.

## Skills
Some skills I've learned by working on this blog is **how to Google**, **problem decompositions**, and **organization**.

#### How to Google
When researching my topics, many sources pop up and I'm obviously not going to sit down and read through all of them. I skim through the titles until I find one that is reliable [so not something like Wikipedia]. Once I find a good source, I open it up and read from there. That way, I'm not taking forever just to research one topic. Also, I was not looking for degrees, careers or the actual equipment considered medical technology, at least not now at my age, so skimming for what I needed was helpful.

#### Problem Decomposition
Now, I have trouble doing large tasks in one go, such as writing a lot on this blog. I usually start feeling less and less motivated to do the task the longer I work on it, which results in less effort being put in. As a result, my grades can start going down, so I have learned to use problem decomposition, which is breaking down a larger task into smaller chunks. That way, it's easier to complete a task without putting in less effort.

#### Organization
Writing a blog should be nice and organized so that it's easy to read. I'v learned to use Markdown to make my blog look neat, that way people aren't reading big chunks of text without knowing what's what. I've learned to use headings, bold text, italics and such to make sure my blog is organized and so people know what they're reading.
