## Finding Information.
I have now moved onto researching medical technology, both software [apps/websites] and hardware [actual equipment]. I'll be honest, finding software for the medical field was a bit difficult, but in the end I found some examples. I didn't actually have to find the apps and websites that doctors use to chart and diagnose, but some apps and websites they might use to aid in care and study. 

I found most of the software from this website, [9 must-have apps for nurses and nursing students](https://www.capella.edu/blog/nursing/must-have-apps-for-nurses-and-nursing-students/) by Capella University.

For the hardware, I found most of it from this webpage, [Top 50 Medical Equipment Items Every Hospital Must Own](https://medigear.uk/blog/top-50-medical-equipment-items-every-hospital-must-own)

Now finding the information for this wasn't easy, I had to revise what I put in the search bar to find what I was looking for. Sometimes, nothing would pop up, so I had to put in a different input and hope that results would show up. Other times, none of the websites gave me the information I needed, so I combed through the best results to find a good source.
